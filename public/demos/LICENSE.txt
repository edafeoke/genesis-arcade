The readable original source is scripts/build-quickdraw.mjs. It generates the
complete Quickdraw Duel ROM deterministically with Node.js. It contains no
commercial game, BIOS, artwork, music or other third-party ROM content.

Two-player instructions:
Press Start on either controller. Wait for the green GO signal, then press B.
The first player to press B wins. Pressing B before GO loses the round.
Press Start for another round. Simultaneous presses in one video frame tie.
Genesis Arcade keyboard: Enter = Start; S = B.
